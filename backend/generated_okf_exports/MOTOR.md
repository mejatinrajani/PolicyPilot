### Clause (2): Priority of claim under section 140
**Summary:** Mandates that a claim for compensation under section 140 for death or permanent disablement must be processed as quickly as possible and, when a concurrent claim exists on the principle of fault, the section 140 claim must be settled first.

**Cross-References:**

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**

### Clause (3): Limitation on double compensation
**Summary:** Specifies that when the same person is liable under both section 140 and the right on the principle of fault, the liability is limited to the excess of the second compensation over the first, if any; otherwise no liability for the second compensation.

**Exceptions & Overrides:**
- Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-section (1)

**Cross-References:**
- Section 140
- Right on the principle of fault (Section 163A)
- Principle of fault (Section 163A)

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> (3) Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-section (1), where in respect of the death or permanent disablement of any person, the person liable to pay compensation under section 140 is also liable to pay compensation in accordance with the right on the principle of fault, the person so liable shall pay the first-mentioned compensation and (a) if the amount of the first-mentioned compensation is less than the amount of the second-mentioned compensation, he shall be liable to pay (in addition to the first-mentioned compensation) only so much of the second-mentioned compensation as is equal to the amount by which it exceeds the first-mentioned compensation; (b) if the amount of the first-mentioned compensation is equal to or more than the amount of the second-mentioned compensation, he shall not be liable to pay the second-mentioned compensation.

### Clause 142: Definition of permanent disablement
**Summary:** Defines permanent disablement for the chapter as injuries resulting from an accident referred to in sub‑section (1) of section 140, covering loss of sight, hearing, limbs, joint powers, or permanent facial disfiguration.

**Cross-References:**
- Section 140 sub‑section (1)

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> 142. Permanent disablement.—For the purposes of this Chapter, permanent disablement of a person shall be deemed to have resulted from an accident of the nature referred to in sub-section (1) of section 140 if such person has suffered by reason of the accident, any injury or injuries involving— (a) permanent privation of the sight of either eye or the hearing of either ear, or privation of any member or joint; or (b) destruction or permanent impairing of the powers of any member or joint; or (c) permanent disfiguration of the head or face.

### Clause Section 150: Transfer of insured's rights to third parties on insolvency
**Summary:** When an insured becomes insolvent, undergoes a winding‑up, or a similar event, any liability incurred by the insured is transferred to and vests in the third party to whom the liability was incurred.

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> 150. Rights of third parties against insurers on insolvency of the insured.—(1) Where under any contract of insurance ... a person is insured against liabilities ... (a) in the event of the person becoming insolvent ... or (b) where the insured person is a company, in the event of a winding up order ... if, either before or after that event, any such liability is incurred by the insured person, his rights against the insurer ... shall ... be transferred to and vest in the third party to whom the liability was so incurred.

### Clause Section 152: Requirement of third‑party participation in settlements
