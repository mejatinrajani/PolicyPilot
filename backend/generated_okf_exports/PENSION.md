### Clause Annexure 2 - Rider Charge Inclusion: Inclusion of Rider Charges in Illustration
**Summary:** Rider charges are included in the illustration unless they are collected via a separate rider premium, in which case they are excluded.

**Exceptions & Overrides:**
- If rider charges are collected via a separate rider premium, they are excluded from the illustration; otherwise they are included.

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> In case rider charges are collected explicitly through collection of rider premium, and not by way of cancellation of units, then, such charges are not considered in this illustration. In other cases, rider charges are included in other charges.
