### Clause (2): Effectual nomination and insurer liability
**Summary:** A nomination is effective only if it is endorsed on the policy and registered by the insurer; it may be cancelled or changed before maturity by endorsement, further endorsement or a will, but the insurer is not liable for payments to a nominee unless written notice of such cancellation or change has been delivered to the insurer.

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Any such nomination in order to be effectual shall, unless it is incorporated in the text of the policy itself, be made by an endorsement on the policy communicated to the insurer and registered by him in the records relating to the policy and any such nomination may at any time before the policy matures for payment be cancelled or changed by an endorsement or a further endorsement or a Will, as the case may be, but unless notice in writing of any such cancellation or change has been delivered to the insurer, the insurer shall not be liable for any payment under the policy made bona fide by him to a nominee mentioned in the text of the policy or registered in records of the insurer.

### Clause (4): Automatic cancellation of nomination upon transfer or assignment
**Summary:** A transfer or assignment of a policy made under section 38 automatically cancels any nomination, except where the assignment is to the insurer as security for a loan or similar arrangements, in which case the nomination is not cancelled but the nominee's rights are limited to the insurer's interest.

**Exceptions & Overrides:**
- Provided that the assignment, of a policy to the insurer who bears the risk on the policy at the time of the assignment, in consideration of a loan granted by that insurer on the security of the policy within its surrender value, or its reassignment on repayment of the loan shall not cancel a nomination, but shall affect the rights of the nominee only to the extent of the insurer's interest in the policy.

**Cross-References:**
- Section 38 of the Insurance Act

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> A transfer or assignment of a policy made in accordance with section 38 shall automatically cancel a nomination: Provided that the assignment, of a policy to the insurer who bears the risk on the policy at the time of the assignment, in consideration of a loan granted by that insurer on the security of the policy within its surrender value, or its reassignment on repayment of the loan shall not cancel a nomination, but shall affect the rights of the nominee only to the extent of the insurer's interest in the policy.

### Clause Section 38(1): Form of Assignment
**Summary:** An assignment or transfer of a life insurance policy must be effected only by an endorsement on the policy or a separate instrument, signed by the transferor/assignor and attested by at least one witness, specifying the transfer details.

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> A transferor assignment of a policy of insurance, wholly or in part, whether with or without consideration, may be made only by an endorsement upon the policy itself or by a separate instrument, signed in either case by the transferor or by the assignor or his duly authorised agent and attested by at least one witness, specifically setting forth the fact of transfer or assignment and the reasons thereof, the antecedents of the assignee and the terms on which the assignment is made.

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
