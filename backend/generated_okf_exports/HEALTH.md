### Clause 10): Renewal of Health Insurance Policy
**Summary:** Health policies are renewable unless the product is withdrawn or there is established fraud, non‑disclosure or misrepresentation; insurer cannot deny renewal due to prior claims and may only underwrite afresh if the sum insured is increased.

**Exceptions & Overrides:**
- except in case of established fraud or non-disclosure or misrepresentation by the Insured.

**Cross-References:**
- Chapter II of this circular

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Renewal of Health Insurance Policy: a. A health insurance policy shall be renewable provided the product is not withdrawn, except in case of established fraud or non-disclosure or misrepresentation by the Insured. If the product is withdrawn, the policyholder shall be provided with suitable options to migrate as per the procedure stated under Chapter II of this circular. b. An Insurer shall not deny the renewal on the ground that the policyholder had made a claim (s) in the preceding policy years. c. An Insurer shall not resort to fresh underwriting unless there is an increase in sum insured. In case increase in sum insured is requested by the policyholder, the Insurer may underwrite only to the extent of increased sum insured.

### Clause 5): Free Look Period
**Summary:** Policyholder may review the policy and cancel within 30 days from receipt of the policy document, provided the policy term is one year or more.

**Statutory Thresholds & Timelines:**
- 30 days

**Exceptions & Overrides:**
- Applicable only to policies with a term of one year or more.

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Free Look Period: A period of 30 days (from the date of receipt of the policy document) is available to the policyholder to review the terms and conditions of the policy. If he/she is not satisfied with any of the terms and conditions, he/she has the option to cancel his/her policy. This option is available in case of policies with a term of one year or more.

### Clause 8): Grace Period for payment of premium
**Summary:** A grace period of 15 days is allowed for monthly premium instalments and 30 days for quarterly, half‑yearly or annual instalments; renewal during the grace period protects all accrued credits.

**Statutory Thresholds & Timelines:**
- fifteen days for monthly instalments
- thirty days for quarterly/half‑yearly/annual instalments

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Grace Period for payment of premium: a) The grace period of fifteen days (where premium is paid on a monthly instalments) and thirty days (where premium is paid in quarterly/half- yearly/annual instalments) is available on the premium due date, to pay the premium. b) If the policy is renewed during grace period, all the credits (sum insured, No Claim Bonus, Specific Waiting periods, waiting periods for pre-existing diseases, Moratorium period etc.) accrued under the policy shall be protected. The same is applicable for both Indemnity and Benefit products.

### Clause Clause 12: Portability of Indemnity Policies
**Summary:** Policyholder may port policies to another insurer; the existing insurer must provide underwriting and claim history within 72 hours and the acquiring insurer must decide and communicate within 5 days of receiving that information.

**Statutory Thresholds & Timelines:**
- not more than 72 hours of receipt of request
- not more than 5 days of receipt of information

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Portability in case of Indemnity Policies: a. A Policyholder has the choice to port his/ her policies from one Insurer to another. The Acquiring and the Existing Insurers shall jointly, ensure that the entire underwriting details and claim history of the Policyholders are seamlessly transferred. b. The existing insurer shall provide the information sought by the Acquiring insurer immediately but not more than 72 hours of receipt of request through Insurance Information Bureau of India (IIB) https://iib.gov.in/ portal. c. The Acquiring insurer shall decide and communicate on the proposal immediately but not more than 5 days of receipt of information from Existing insurer. d. The policyholder is entitled to transfer the credits gained to the extent of the Sum Insured, No Claim Bonus, specific waiting periods, waiting period for pre-existing disease , Moratorium period etc from the Existing Insurer to the Acquiring Insurer in the previous policy.

### Clause Clause 13: Non‑contestability after Moratorium Period
**Summary:** Health policies and claims cannot be contested on grounds of non‑disclosure or misrepresentation after a 60‑month continuous coverage moratorium, except in cases of established fraud.

**Statutory Thresholds & Timelines:**
- 60 months of continuous coverage

**Exceptions & Overrides:**
- except for established fraud

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Policy/Claim cannot be contested: No policy and claim of health insurance shall be contestable on any grounds of non-disclosure and/or misrepresentation except for established fraud, after the completion of the Moratorium Period, i.e. 60 months of continuous coverage Note :The accrued credits gained under the ported and migrated policies shall be counted for the purpose of calculating the Moratorium period.

### Clause Clause 15: Cashless Facility Approval
**Summary:** Insurers must aim for 100 % cashless settlement, decide cashless authorisation within one hour of request, and implement required systems by 31 July 2024; reimbursements are allowed only in exceptional circumstances.

**Statutory Thresholds & Timelines:**
- not more than one hour of receipt of request
- not later than 31 July 2024

**Exceptions & Overrides:**
- only in exceptional circumstances

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Approval for Cashless facility: a. Every insurer shall strive to achieve 100% cashless claim settlement in a time bound manner. The insurers shall endeavor to ensure that the instances of claims being settled through reimbursement are at bare minimum and only in exceptional circumstances. b. Insurer shall decide on the request for cashless authorization immediately but not more than one hour of receipt of request. Necessary systems and procedures shall be put in place by the Insurer immediately and not later than 31 st July, 2024. c. Insurers may arrange for dedicated Help Desks in physical mode at the hospital to deal and assist with the cashless requests. d. Insurers shall also provide pre-authorization to the policyholder through Digital mode.

### Clause Clause 16: Final Authorization for Hospital Discharge
**Summary:** Insurers must grant final discharge authorisation within three hours of the hospital’s request; any delay beyond three hours makes the insurer liable for any extra charges incurred.

**Statutory Thresholds & Timelines:**
- within three hours of the receipt of discharge authorization request

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Final authorization for Discharge from the hospital: a. Insurer shall grant final authorization within three hours of the receipt of discharge authorization request from the hospital. In no case, the policyholder shall be made to wait to be discharged from the Hospital. b. If there is any delay beyond three hours, the additional amount if any charged by the hospital shall be borne by the insurer from shareholder's fund. c. In the event of the death of the policyholder during the treatment, the insurer shall: i. immediately process the request for claim settlement. ii. get the mortal remains (dead body) released from the hospital immediately

### Clause Clause 17: Settlement of Claims – Review Committee
**Summary:** No claim may be repudiated without approval of the PMC or its three‑member Claims Review Committee; repudiated or partially disallowed claims must be communicated with full details and reference to policy terms, and insurers/TPAs must collect required documents from hospitals without burdening the policyholder.

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Settlement of Claims: a) No claim shall be repudiated without the approval of PMC or a three- member sub-group of PMC called the Claims Review Committee (CRC). b) In case, the claim is repudiated or disallowed partially, details shall be conveyed to the claimant along with full details giving reference to the specific terms and conditions of the policy document. c) Pursuant to intimation of the claim, Insurers and Third Party Administrators (TPAs) shall collect the required documents from the Hospitals. Policyholder shall not be required to submit the documents.

### Clause Clause 18: Claims Across Multiple Indemnity Policies
**Summary:** When a policyholder holds several indemnity policies, they may file a claim under any one; the insurer of the chosen policy acts as primary and must coordinate with other insurers to settle any balance amount.

**Verbatim Regulatory Text:**
> Claims in respect of multiple Policies held by policyholders: a) Indemnity Policies: A policyholder can file for claim settlement as per his/her choice under any policy. The Insurer of that chosen policy shall be treated as the primary Insurer. In case the available coverage under the said policy is less than the admissible claim amount, the primary Insurer shall seek the details of other available policies of the policyholder and shall coordinate with other Insurers to ensure settlement of the balance amount as per the policy

### Clause Manmohan Nanda v. United India Assurance Co. Ltd. (2022) 4 SCC 582: Non-Disclosure Repudiation Principle
**Summary:** An insurer cannot repudiate a claim on the ground of non-disclosure where the alleged pre-existing medical condition has no causal connection to the illness for which the policyholder is hospitalized; the insurer must establish a direct, material link.
